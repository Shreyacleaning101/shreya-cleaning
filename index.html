FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How can I book a cleaning service?
Select your package, home size, preferred date and location in the enquiry form. Then click "Send Enquiry on WhatsApp". Your selected information will be automatically added to the WhatsApp message.
Which areas do you serve?
We provide cleaning services in Mumbai and surrounding areas. Please send your exact location on WhatsApp so we can confirm service availability.
Can I request additional cleaning services?
Yes. You can select additional services such as kitchen, bathroom, sofa, window, balcony and extra deep cleaning in the enquiry form.
Can I call instead of using WhatsApp?
Yes. You can call Shreya Cleaning Services directly using the Call button on the website.
How will I know the final price?
After receiving your requirements, location and home details, our team can confirm the applicable service details and pricing.
Can I request a specific date?
Yes. Select your preferred date in the enquiry form. Availability can then be confirmed with our team on WhatsApp.
