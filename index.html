Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our cleaning services.

**Summary:** Do you bring your own equipment and materials?

Yes. Our team comes with professional cleaning equipment and all required materials. You don’t need to arrange anything from your side.

**Summary:** How long does a deep cleaning usually take?

A 1 BHK typically takes 4–6 hours depending on the condition of the house. Larger homes or heavy kitchen/bathroom cleaning may take longer. We always confirm the approximate time when giving the quote.

**Summary:** Do I need to be present at home during cleaning?

It is preferred that someone is present, especially for the first visit. If you cannot stay, please inform us in advance so we can plan accordingly.

**Summary:** Are the cleaning products safe for kids and pets?

Yes, we use effective and home-safe cleaning methods. If you have any specific preference (like mild or fragrance-free products), just tell us while booking.

**Summary:** How do I book and make the payment?

Just message us on WhatsApp or fill the form on this website. We confirm the date, time and final quote. Payment is collected after the service is completed to your satisfaction.

**Summary:** Which areas of Mumbai do you serve?

We are based in Thakur Village, Kandivali East and regularly serve Kandivali, Borivali, Malad, Goregaon, Andheri and nearby areas. For other locations, just message us — we often cover them too.
