Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our cleaning services.

Do you provide both residential and commercial cleaning?

Yes. We provide home deep cleaning and commercial cleaning services. Use the separate enquiry forms to share your requirements.

How can I get a quotation for home cleaning?

Complete the Residential Enquiry form with your home size, location, service and preferred date. The details will open in WhatsApp.

How can an office or company request a quotation?

Use the Commercial Enquiry form. You can share the office area, workstations, chairs, washrooms, pantries and other relevant details.

Do you clean furnished and empty flats?

Yes. Share the property condition and the work required so our team can discuss the suitable scope.

Can I request recurring office cleaning?

Yes. The commercial form includes options for one-time, weekly, monthly and twice-a-month cleaning requirements.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve customers in Mumbai. Share your exact area or business location to confirm availability.

Are all services covered by the same inclusions?

No. The agreed scope depends on the selected service, property condition and specific requirements. Our team will clarify the work before booking.
