FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to common questions about our cleaning services.

What cleaning services do you provide?

We provide full home deep cleaning, kitchen cleaning, bathroom cleaning, sofa cleaning, mattress cleaning, office deep cleaning and commercial cleaning.

Do you provide residential and corporate cleaning?

Yes. We provide cleaning services for residential properties, offices, commercial establishments and workplaces.

Can I book kitchen cleaning separately?

Yes. Kitchen deep cleaning can be booked separately. You do not need to select your home size.

Can I book bathroom cleaning separately?

Yes. You can request bathroom deep cleaning separately by submitting a residential enquiry.

Do you provide sofa cleaning?

Yes. We provide sofa deep cleaning. Share the number of sofas and your requirements with our team.

How can I request an office cleaning quotation?

Use our corporate enquiry form. Share your company name, contact details, full address, property area and preferred cleaning date.

How is the cleaning service quotation decided?

Our team reviews the property size, condition, service requirements and scope of work before sharing a quotation.

When is my booking confirmed?

Sending a WhatsApp enquiry does not automatically confirm your booking. Our team will contact you to confirm availability and service details.

Which locations do you serve?

We provide cleaning services in Mumbai. Contact us with your location to confirm service availability.
