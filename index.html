Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before you book.

Which areas do you provide cleaning services in?

We serve customers in Mumbai. Please share your area with us so our team can confirm availability.

How can I book a cleaning service?

Choose your service in the enquiry form, enter your details and click Send Enquiry on WhatsApp. Your WhatsApp app will open with the message ready to send.

Do you provide home and office cleaning?

Yes. We provide home deep cleaning and office cleaning services. Contact us with your requirements for a quotation.

Do you clean furnished and empty flats?

Yes. We handle furnished and unfurnished property cleaning. The work scope is discussed according to the property condition.

What details should I share for a quotation?

Share your location, preferred date, property or home size and the areas that need cleaning. For offices, share the approximate area and cleaning requirements.

Can I enquire about a customised cleaning service?

Yes. Select Other Cleaning Service in the form and describe what you need in the additional details section.

Are all cleaning inclusions the same for every property?

Not necessarily. Inclusions depend on the selected service, property condition and agreed scope. Our team will clarify the details before confirmation.
