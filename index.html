Quick answers before you book

**Summary:** How do I book?

Select a service above, add your area (and size if needed), then tap “Send on WhatsApp”. Or call +91 93218 79581.

**Summary:** Which areas do you cover?

We serve across Mumbai. Base is Kandivali East (Thakur Village). Share your location and we’ll confirm coverage.

**Summary:** What’s included in full home cleaning?

Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchen (platform, cabinets exterior, sink, stove), bathrooms, windows, floors and accessible surfaces. Exact scope depends on condition — we’ll clarify before starting.

**Summary:** Do you clean sofas?

Yes. Mention number of sofas/seats and any stains in your enquiry for an accurate quote.

**Summary:** How long does it take?

Depends on size and condition. A typical 1–2 BHK deep clean takes several hours. We’ll estimate once we know details.

**Summary:** How is pricing decided?

Based on service type, size, condition and add-ons. Send an enquiry — we’ll share a clear quote before you confirm.

**Summary:** Empty flat / move-in cleaning?

Yes. Select “Custom” and mention it’s empty or move-in/move-out so we can plan properly.
