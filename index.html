What is the difference between furnished and unfurnished cleaning?

Furnished flat cleaning is for homes containing furniture and household items. Unfurnished flat cleaning is designed for empty properties without furniture.

Standard cleaning includes floor cleaning, bathroom deep cleaning, kitchen exterior cleaning, furniture dusting and other agreed areas.
