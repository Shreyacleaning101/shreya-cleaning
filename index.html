Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a cleaning service.

How can I book a cleaning service?

Select your cleaning service on our website, enter your details and click Send Enquiry on WhatsApp. Our team will contact you to discuss your requirements.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve homes and offices across Mumbai. Share your location with us on WhatsApp and our team will confirm service availability.

What types of homes do you clean?

We provide cleaning for 1 RK, 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK homes, including furnished and unfurnished properties.

Do you provide kitchen and bathroom cleaning separately?

Yes. You can book kitchen deep cleaning and bathroom deep cleaning as individual services.

Do you provide sofa cleaning?

Yes. We provide sofa and upholstery cleaning. Please mention the number of seats, fabric type if known and any specific stains when enquiring.

Do you provide office deep cleaning?

Yes. We clean workstations, chairs, floors, cabins, cupboards, conference rooms, pantries, washrooms and other agreed office areas.

How long does home deep cleaning take?

Duration depends on the home size, condition and selected services. Our team can provide an estimated time after understanding your requirements.

Can I book a cleaning service for a future date?

Yes. Mention your preferred cleaning date in the enquiry form. Our team will check availability and confirm the booking.

How is the service quotation decided?

Our quotation depends on the service type, property size, condition, quantity and specific requirements. We share the quotation before you confirm your booking.

Do you clean empty flats?

Yes. We provide empty flat, move-in and move-out cleaning services. Select Empty Flat Cleaning and share your requirements.
