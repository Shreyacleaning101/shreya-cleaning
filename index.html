What's included in home deep cleaning?

Our team focuses on the main areas of your home. Exact inclusions may vary according to property condition and selected service.

🛋️

Living room & bedrooms

  • Floor cleaning and scrubbing
  • Dusting of accessible furniture
  • Doors, frames and handles
  • Accessible switchboards
  • Cobweb removal
🍽️

Kitchen

  • Platform and backsplash
  • Cabinet exterior surfaces
  • Sink and taps
  • Stove exterior cleaning
  • Accessible grease and dirt removal
🚿

Bathrooms

  • Floor and wall tiles
  • Toilet and wash basin
  • Shower area
  • Taps and fittings
  • Removal of accessible dirt and buildup
🪟

Windows & doors

  • Accessible window glass
  • Window frames and tracks
  • Doors and handles
  • Accessible corners and surfaces
🌿

Balcony & common areas

  • Balcony floor cleaning
  • Accessible surfaces
  • Dust and cobweb removal
🧹

Finishing touches

  • General dust removal
  • Accessible surface wiping
  • Final floor cleaning

Please note – exclusions

  • Removal of paint, cement, construction debris or stubborn permanent stains.
  • Heavy furniture shifting or dismantling.
  • External high-rise glass cleaning or work at unsafe heights.
  • Deep appliance cleaning unless selected as an add-on.
  • Any service not specifically included in the confirmed booking.

Frequently asked questions

Need to know more?

How do I book a home deep cleaning service?

Select your home size, property condition and add-ons above. Enter your details and continue to WhatsApp to send your enquiry.

Are the prices final?

The website shows estimated prices. Our team will confirm the final service scope, property condition, availability and amount before confirming your booking.

Do you clean furnished homes?

Yes. You can select furnished or unfurnished while creating your home cleaning enquiry.

Can I book kitchen or bathroom deep cleaning separately?

Yes. Use the separate enquiry form on the Kitchen Deep Cleaning or Bathroom Deep Cleaning service card. You do not need to select a home size.

Can I book sofa deep cleaning separately?

Yes. Select Sofa Deep Cleaning and share the number of sofas in the separate enquiry form.

Do you provide office deep cleaning?

Yes. Use the commercial enquiry form to share your office address, area, preferred date and additional requirements.

When is my booking confirmed?

Your WhatsApp message is an enquiry. Our team will contact you to confirm availability and final details.

Which areas do you serve?

We provide cleaning services in Mumbai. Contact us with your location to confirm service availability.
